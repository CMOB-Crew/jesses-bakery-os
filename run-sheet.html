Rough edges

Known, and better steered than stumbled into.

Cold start

Load the Overview five minutes before she joins. Warm it renders in about four seconds; on a cold server it has been slower. Opening it once keeps it warm for the session.

34.3 vs 34.5

The panel says 34.3%, the tile below says 34.5%. Same stores, same week, 83 units apart — the engine scored the 1,189 lines it planned, the view scores everything those stores received. The caption says “at the last engine run”. If she spots it, that is the answer.

Accuracy

Don't open it unprompted. It reads 91%, which next to 34.5% waste invites a fair question. It is a backtest of the baseline the plan has to beat, not a score for the plan. Fine if asked, distracting if volunteered.

On-hand

Still off. The ledger doesn't reconcile — 23% of store-days hold more than the shelf physically fits, and 72% of the stock on it sits on lines that sold nothing all week. If she asks whether the system knows what's on the shelf: not yet, and we won't switch it on until it's right.

Ask her this

“Who looks after the DNS for jessesbakery.com.au?” Thirty seconds, and it unblocks the one thing that stops anyone logging in on 3 Sept. Auth email currently sends from a Supabase address at two emails an hour, project-wide — onboarding ten staff would take five hours in two-person batches, half of them landing in junk. Fix costs nothing and takes an hour, but it needs three DNS records on their domain. Ask, get a name, and it's done this week. Don't explain the mechanics to her; just get the name.

Xero

Still the only unscoped leg, and the real risk to 3 Sept. What's needed from her, concretely: the product list with exact codes, the tier pricing formula, and a walk-through of how she places a one-off order today, on paper, start to finish.

How to close

Three things to leave in the room, in this order.

One. The system is measuring her whole network honestly for the first time — including telling her, plainly, which 170 stores it cannot see. That's the part worth trusting.

Two. Coles is the biggest single thing standing between her and the full picture, it has been down 22 days, and the fix is on their pipeline. She needs to push it, and she needs to know what to push for.

Three. Xero is the last unscoped piece before 3 September. Get the product list, the pricing formula, and the paper walk-through of a one-off order — in the meeting, not after it.
